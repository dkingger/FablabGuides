Prusa MK3S

The MK3S is a solid and versatile 3D printer from Prusa, suitable for both beginners and experienced users. It is known for its reliability, high quality, and automatic features.

How to use a Prusa MK3S

Change filament

Start print

Tips and tricks

  • Keep the build plate clean – use isopropyl alcohol and a microfiber cloth.
  • Adjust the first layer correctly – use Z-offset and test print.
  • Listen for unusual sounds during printing – it may indicate problems.
  • If the filament does not stick, stop and clean the nozzle and build plate.
