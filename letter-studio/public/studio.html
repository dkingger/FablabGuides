Fit & construction

The gap is applied around every face edge. Recess moves the face and ledge down without changing body depth.

Printed trim cap

Zero is nominal contact, negative is tighter, positive is looser. Calibrate before production; this is friction retention, not a snap latch.

The acrylic seats on the body ledge. The cap lip overlaps its front edge; the skirt slips over the body. Total depth includes the front lip. Inner openings may have separate cap rings.

Bond the acrylic to the cap with a compatible adhesive. Secure the cap with adhesive or shop-drilled retaining screws after testing the fit. This version does not generate locking features.

Back attachment

The collar presses into the rear opening; the cover stops at the rim. No bosses or holes. Start with a fit test: zero is nominal contact, negative is tighter, positive is looser. Retention depends on the print and material.

Removable back

Flat cover rests on the rear rim. Screws enter from the back into unthreaded, blind pilot holes. Use pan-head screws; no countersinks or threaded inserts are generated. Match the pilot to your actual screw and test in your filament.

Short bosses sit near the rear with angled supports into the wall. A clear gap remains behind the face. Check actual lighting and screw fit on one letter.
