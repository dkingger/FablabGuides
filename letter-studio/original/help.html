From first design
to first print.

Keep this guide beside the studio. Start with one small assembly, then increase complexity.

Start with a project

The welcome window offers four starting points: Front-lit signs, Backlit letters, Decorative letters and Design your own. Hover over a picture, focus it with the keyboard, or press its information button for an explanation. Choose a card to open that workspace.

Use Projects or Explore all projects to reopen the chooser. The sidebar project menu uses the same pictures. Skip this screen next time remembers your preference and last-used section; it does not save your artwork. Front-lit, canvas and decorative workspaces can save an editable design to your device. Backlit currently exports print files and installation templates only; it does not save an editable project. Keep that tab open until you have exported.

Your first printed letter

  1. Open Examples in the studio and load Your first printed letter. Loading replaces the current design; Undo restores it.
  2. In Design, use one bold letter. In Size, start at 100 mm high and 35 mm deep; enter your printer’s usable bed size.
  3. In Build, choose Separate face and 3D-print the face. Check the face thickness against the intended diffuser material.
  4. Choose Show assembly and open How the parts fit · cross-section. The face sits on a ledge; this construction has no automatic click lock.
  5. Skip lighting for the first fit test. In Print, prepare full-detail print files. Resolve any blocked conditions.
  6. Download the ZIP or arrange the multi-plate project. Read the instructions and inspect each sliced layer. Print and check fit before repeating the design.

In Find the right fit · guided test, generate sample clearances when available, print them, and apply the allowance that fits your printer and material.

Choose how the parts assemble

Blue: body. Green: face. Amber: removable cap or back. These schematics explain assembly; the live cross-section uses your dimensions.

Inset lettering · wide border

Smaller letter faces fit inside a wider body with shared space for LEDs and wires.

Smaller letter-shaped faces fit into a wider body. Printed inserts have hollow skirts; print face-down. There is no continuous diffuser or mask. Face height can be recessed or raised. The skirt locates the insert; it is not a click lock.

Flanged face

Printed face with a flange fitting inside or outside the body. Test fit before use.

In Build, choose Flanged face, then Face flange → Inside or Outside. Set flange depth, wall and colour; expand Fit and printing for per-side allowance and entry chamfer. Print the body back-down and the face face-down with the flange growing upwards. Calibrate the fit; no snap latch. Contour lightboxes also have a separate bonded mask.

Separate face · closed back

Insert the face, then secure it. No removable rear cover.

Body back-down; face flat. The face rests on an internal ledge. A sliding fit alone does not lock it in place; plan retention and test your actual assembly.

Full-depth removable sleeve

Outer sides cover the whole letter/logo down to the back; sleeve and bonded face lift off together.

Print the sleeve lip-down. Bond the face to its lip, then fit the sleeve over the body. The full-depth version covers the outer sides down to the back. Retention relies on friction; calibrate before bonding. Older editor builds use a shorter removable frame.

Removable rear tray

Bond the face to the shell; remove the tray for rear access.

Print the shell front-down and rear tray floor-down. Bond the face into the shell. The rear tray locates inside the shell; test fit and retention.

Acrylic face + trim cap

A printed cap overlaps the cut acrylic face.

Print the body and cap separately. Cut the acrylic from the supplied outline. The cap overlaps the acrylic. Inner openings may create separate cap rings.

Face and walls printed together

Print front-down; attach a separate rear cover.

Print front and walls together, front-down. A separate rear cover closes the body. Choose screw fastening or a calibrated friction collar. Not supported for lightboxes.

Make a box around a shape or logo

  1. Choose Shapes, then a gallery shape; or choose SVG file and the adjacent Browse button.
  2. Select Contour lightbox. Its border expands when needed to connect separate elements. Check the resulting finished size.
  3. Choose the construction and face method. Standard lightboxes have a continuous diffuser and an opaque mask; inset lettering uses individual inserts instead.
  4. Inspect the assembly before exporting. For printed mask/diffuser stacks, follow the colour-change instructions or use the separate parts; do not print both alternatives.

Add rear wire passages

Open Mounting and choose Add wire passages. LED-strip placement, strip profiles and consumption calculations are hidden for the first release.

  1. Open Edit wire holes · large preview and select the body.
  2. Set the hole diameter. Choose Place rear wire hole, then click inside the body. Drag the purple circle to adjust its position.
  3. Choose Apply & check. Accepted holes are cut through the body bottom or removable back/tray. Correct red blocked holes before downloading.
  4. Prepare full-detail parts again after changes. The ZIP includes wire-passages with a diagram, hole coordinates and instructions. Inspect the actual bores in the slicer.

Blue marks show existing desk-base passages. Leave clearance for the actual cable or connector. Rear entries do not create sidewall tunnels between separate letters. Adding wire passages does not calculate a lighting layout or electrical design.

Desk mounting without screws

In Mounting, choose the wired desk base, then Without screws · slide-in click key · test first. The PLA-R3 prototype uses rigid feet and separate horizontal locking keys, allowing a 20 mm base.

  1. Print the foot, socket, separate locking key and cover-corner samples before the full sign. Use plain PLA for the key; print it flat in the exported orientation.
  2. Drop the foot into its socket. From underneath, squeeze the key tips, lift the key into place and slide it right until both catches engage.
  3. To release, remove the bottom cover, squeeze the key tips together, slide left 6.5 mm and lower the key out. Then lift the sign.

Clearance adjusts the rigid guide fit, not spring stiffness. The bottom cover uses a separate friction fit. This connector remains a prototype: fit, fatigue, retention and stability need a physical print test. The alternative quarter-turn trials are separate test files and are not part of this release.

Friction fit remains available for the original 10 mm feet. Zero allowance is nominal contact; positive is looser and negative is tighter. For every method, the base must contain all sockets. Test tipping with the completed sign and support the base when carrying it.

Print a design larger than the bed

In Size → Split a large design, select butterfly keys or integral male/female dovetails. Current support is limited to compatible connected bodies with printed Separate front or Inset lettering, split left to right. Desk bases and cut-acrylic split faces are not supported.

  1. Enter usable bed dimensions and a margin.
  2. Choose cuts between artwork shapes, or even sections when connected script has no gaps.
  3. Prepare full-detail files and choose View cut parts & assembly. Check labels and assembly order.
  4. Print the small joint test before the full sections. Dovetails resist separation across the seam; vertical retention depends on friction, not a latch.
  5. Use the labelled assembly map and instructions in the ZIP. If a joint is blocked, change the dimensions or seam strategy; do not assume a missing joint is safe.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

The preview is blank or slow

Wait for the status beneath the model title. Complex imports take longer. Try a simple bundled letter; if it works, inspect the imported outlines and small details. Enable browser hardware acceleration if WebGL is unavailable. The last valid model may remain while an edit is calculated; it is not the new result.

The SVG does not import

Use closed, filled outlines. Convert text and strokes to paths in your vector editor. Remove embedded bitmap images, filters and unsupported effects. Read the specific import diagnostics. The current limits include a 1 MB file and a 40,000 sampled-point budget. Reduce genuinely excessive detail without deleting necessary holes.

The face fits, but falls out

A locating ledge or skirt is not necessarily a lock. Check the construction description: some faces require adhesive, a retaining cap or another attachment. Do not assume a friction fit remains secure after repeated removal or heating.

A construction or splitting option is unavailable

The current combination may trap removable parts, lack a supported export, or leave too little space for a joint. Read the explanation beside the control. Splitting currently works only left to right and requires supported printed-face constructions.

Nothing happens when I download

Prepare full-detail files first and resolve any highlighted errors. For multi-plate output, arrange the plates. Check the browser’s download list (Ctrl+J on Windows). Changing the design invalidates the previous files; prepare them again. Use Feedback to download a diagnostic report if it still fails.

The face or a part seems missing

Open the ZIP and check the parts list. Printed faces are separate unless the construction integrates the face and walls. CNC/laser faces export as cutting outlines. For a lightbox, choose the combined printed face stack or its separate alternatives. Inspect every plate of a multi-plate project.

Where are the LED-strip options?

They are hidden for the first release while the layout planner is improved. Wire-passage placement remains available. Choose and position your lighting separately, then test it with your actual diffuser and materials.

Can I save and reopen my design?

Yes. Use Save design at the top of the studio to download a .bogstavvaerkstedet.json file, then Open design to reopen it. It includes text or SVG artwork, settings, wire-hole positions, glyph adjustments and any uploaded custom font. Share custom fonts only when their licence permits it. Opening a file can be undone. There is no cloud saving or automatic backup; keep your downloaded file. The browser may warn before leaving with unsaved changes, but this is not recovery from a crash. Fabrication ZIPs and 3MFs cannot be reopened as editable projects.

Canvas text and patterned faces

Design your own opens a canvas for text, SVG and shapes. Select a text object, edit its words, font or spacing, then press Apply text to update its outline. Pending text stays in the controls until approved. Ctrl/Command + Enter also applies text.

Apply changes includes all pending text edits and checks the complete 3D model before returning to Build. If a check fails, the canvas stays open and the previous applied model remains available. Read the correction, change the indicated setting and try again. Cancel discards unapplied canvas edits.

In Pattern, choose a shape and adjust Hole size, Space between holes and Solid border. Keep whole omits holes crossing the border; Trim to fit clips them. The guide previews the layout; Apply checks the actual face and fittings. For a dense pattern, choose Auto space holes. If no holes fit, reduce Hole size or Solid border, or enlarge the artwork. Use solid face removes the holes. The Details disclosure retains the technical message. Inspect the exported face and print a small sample.

Backlit and decorative letters

Backlit letters include printable standoffs, editable mounting positions and installation templates. Every connected part needs at least three valid mounts. Size & fit checks room for the selected strip and mounting sockets; it can suggest a checked larger size. Dark bodies can include a separate white reflector insert. Test fitting and illumination with the actual filament and LEDs.

Decorative letters provide solid, textured, tray and personalized styles. Their download buttons are in the decorative workspace. Print a small texture or fit test before a full-size design.

Report a problem or a print result

Use Feedback in the studio. Describe what you expected, what happened, and the steps to reproduce it. For print results, add printer, filament, layer height and whether the fit was tight, loose or correct.

Review and download the report, then attach it when sharing with the beta organiser. Nothing is submitted automatically. Artwork and screenshots are only shared if you add them yourself. You can submit your feedback through the beta feedback form. The form opens on Google Forms. Review your answers and press Submit there; downloaded diagnostics are not attached automatically.
