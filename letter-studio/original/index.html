BEFORE YOUR FIRST PRINT

A few useful answers.

Explore the full help guide ↗
Is Bogstavværkstedet free?

Yes. The current beta includes free design and fabrication downloads without an account. You can save an editable design file to your device; cloud project storage is not included.

Can I use a Bambu Lab or another 3D printer?

Use the multi-plate 3MF project with Bambu Studio, or individual STL files with your preferred slicer. Choose your printer, filament and settings there. Downloads contain geometry, not ready-to-run G-code.

Can I turn a logo into a lightbox?

You can import supported SVG artwork and create a contour enclosure. Convert text and strokes to closed, filled paths first. Start with simple artwork and inspect small details and interior openings.

Does the app plan the LED layout?

LED placement and consumption calculations are not available in this release. Rear wire-passage placement is available. Plan your lighting separately and test it with your actual face material.

Do I need glue or screws?

It depends on the construction. Some parts locate with a friction fit; others need bonding or fastening. A face that fits a ledge is not automatically locked in place. Check the assembly notes and test retention.
