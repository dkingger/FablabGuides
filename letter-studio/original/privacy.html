WEBSITE & STUDIO

Your artwork.
Your device.

Bogstavværkstedet processes typed lettering, uploaded SVG artwork and uploaded fonts in your browser. The studio does not upload these files to a cloud project store. Save design downloads an editable file to your device containing artwork, settings and any uploaded custom font. Keep your own copy: there is no automatic backup.

Optional printed-sign quote requests

Normal design and download actions remain local. If you choose Request a printed sign, the app creates a fixed copy of your production files, editable design (including embedded fonts), preview and request details. Download request package saves that information to your device; it does not send it. Where live submission is enabled, Send quote request uploads that package and your name, email, delivery details and notes privately to the shop for a manual quote. A receipt reference confirms storage, not an accepted order, payment, production approval or email delivery. The shop reviews files before quoting. No marketing subscription is created. Do not share designs or fonts you lack permission to share. You can contact the organiser through the feedback form about a request or deletion, quoting its reference.

Website analytics and hosting

The public editor includes Google Analytics to measure website usage. Google Analytics can use cookies and process usage and device information. Feature events record the project type chosen, print-file preparation, requested exports, design-file opening canvas approval and quote-flow actions (opened, package download requested, submission started, failed or received), with the app version and preparation duration where relevant. These feature events do not include your lettering, SVG artwork, fonts, filenames or raw error messages. Feature-event sending is disabled in downloaded previews, on localhost and when the browser signals Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. A download event records the request, not confirmation that you saved or printed the file. This is separate from the studio’s local geometry processing. Google’s handling of this information is described in Google’s privacy policy. The public website is hosted through Web Hosting Canada; hosting services may process connection data and access logs to deliver and protect the website.

Feedback you choose to share

The beta feedback form is hosted on Google Forms. Your answers are sent to the form owner when you submit the form. Providing an email address is optional. Share only the artwork, screenshots and information you intend the owner to see.

Local diagnostic reports

The studio keeps up to 50 technical events in memory, such as preview duration and preparation failures. Reloading clears them. You can review and download a feedback report with an optional technical summary. Downloading a report does not send it to the form or automatically upload your artwork.

Questions about your feedback

Use the feedback form to contact the beta organiser about a previous submission. Include enough detail to identify it, and an email address if you would like a reply.
