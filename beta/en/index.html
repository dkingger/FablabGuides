FabLab is an open workshop for learning, experiments and prototypes.

You find us in Spinderihallerne in Vejle. Machines are free to use during open FabLab, while materials are charged per use. Instructors and volunteers help both new and experienced users.

Opening hours Tuesday 15:00–20:00

With some exceptions during holidays and special events.
