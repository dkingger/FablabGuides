Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

This statement describes the accessibility of FabLab Guides across guide pages, material pages and online tools.

Last updated: 26.09.2026

Our goal

We want the site to be usable by as many people as possible, regardless of device, input method or ability.

We aim for ongoing improvements in line with WCAG 2.2 level AA.

Status

The site is partially aligned with accessibility principles. Much of the navigation, contrast and responsive layout works well, but there are still areas we continue to improve.

  • Some interactive elements may need stronger focus states in all views.
  • Some image series and custom widgets still need extra keyboard navigation.
  • Some older subpages may still lack consistent alternative text.

Feedback

If you run into barriers on the site, we would very much like to hear from you so we can fix issues as quickly as possible.

You can contact the FabLab Guides team, and we prioritise issues that affect access to content or navigation.
