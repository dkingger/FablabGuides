About us

Fablab Spinderihallerne is an open workshop environment where you can work with digital fabrication, electronics, prototypes, and creative development.

Every Tuesday from 15:00 – 20:00 (with some exceptions) there is open FabLab where everyone is welcome. The machines are free to use, but you must pay for materials. Our instructors and our team of volunteers will help show you how to operate the machines.

You can find us at Spinderihallerne in Vejle.
