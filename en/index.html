New users

First time in FabLab?

You are welcome at open FabLab every Tuesday from 3:00 PM to 8:00 PM.

You do not need to be an expert. Instructors and volunteers help you get started with machines, materials and files.

It is free to use the machines, but you pay for the materials you use.

Feel free to bring:

  • your idea, sketch or file
  • your own computer, if you want to continue working on the design
  • a little patience if there is a queue at the machines

FabLab is an open workshop for learning, experiments and prototypes.

You find us in our open workshop. Machines are free to use during open FabLab, while materials are charged per use. Instructors and volunteers help both new and experienced users.
